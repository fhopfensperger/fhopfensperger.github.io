About

I am a Lead Engineer at Allianz Technology in Munich and based in Landshut, building internal platforms and cloud-native systems with an emphasis on platform APIs, developer experience, and operational clarity.

My background spans enterprise architecture, consulting, and platform delivery across Allianz Technology, IBM, Unify, and Siemens.
